One Dataset, Four Views

150 recent food safety inspections from Prince George's County, Maryland, shown four different ways. Each view emphasises different parts of the information and serves a different person.

Which view is for whom?

  • Card View: a diner browsing one place at a time
  • Table View: a health inspector scanning and sorting exact values to plan visits
  • Category View: a reporter comparing types of business, with the inspections tucked inside each group
  • Charts View: anyone who wants the big picture at a glance, with a sentence describing every chart

Data: Prince George's County Open Data, food facility inspections. A "failed check" means one of the 15 food safety checks was marked "Out of Compliance".
